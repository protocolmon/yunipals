## Exact policy projection

`expected_target(original_target)` delegates to the policy's authenticated
one-function projection. `expected_catalog(original_catalog)` changes exactly
one recorded function digest, selected by the policy's complete function OID,
schema, name, `boolean` arguments and old MD5. All other fields, roles, grants,
objects, view hashes, effective privileges and all-column records remain in the
comparison. The policy supplies and verifies both complete function definitions,
their SHA256/MD5 values, and the exact 90-to-120 operand change.

Live target and catalog projections require the actual **amended** body/digest.
They do not accept the old body between policy bookends. `catalog_expectation`
distinguishes an authenticated historical admission that needs its one digest
amendment from a genuinely new committed postgraph that already records the
exact amended digest. This is expected-record handling, not a current proof.

`current(host=None)` executes the new policy's actual database-only current
capture and authenticates the same immutable policy ACK before and after.
The policy leaf does not qualify a source actor or the whole role graph;
the original Source and READ validators continue to do that work.

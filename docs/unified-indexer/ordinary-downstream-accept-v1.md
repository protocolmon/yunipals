current-source receipt. The original private inputs, owned intents, process
epochs, catalog checks and fresh reviews remain required.

The reader route is the immutable `acknowledgement(result_sha, manifest_sha)`
from `ypal-ordinary-reader-accept-v2.py` (`973cc759…`) and its actual source

- `reader`: `resultSha256`, `manifestSha256`.
- `feature`: `resultSha256`, `manifestSha256`, `inputSha256`, `producerSha256`,
